Palazzo trousers for Wildberries

Freelance · fashion · 2 main images, 4 funnel shots, retouching

Challenge
For Bless You Today trousers: two main-image options without infographics, built for click-through, plus funnel shots. Fabric, colour and hardware had to stay exactly as in the source photos. Separately: recolour a trench coat to a swatch and remove an extra seam.
Solution
Competitors shoot palazzo trousers on grey with a big caption, so they blend together in the feed. Both main images are full-length with no text: a light studio and an old-town street in warm evening light — easy to A/B test. AI built the scene and the top; I transferred the trousers from the source photo and matched colour and light by hand. The funnel answers pre-purchase questions: volume and fit, details, back and side views, three outfits. The coat was recoloured through a mask keeping folds and texture, the seam removed, and the image upscaled to 1800×2400.
Result
A full card set in about 7 hours: 2 main images, 4 funnel shots and a retouched coat. The trousers in every frame are the real ones from the source photos.

Reels editing for brands and experts

Freelance · 5 videos · 9:16

Challenge
Videos for different clients: a re-edit and a “no-camera video” for an aesthetic medicine clinic, UGC for the embroidery brand BVRLY, a talking head for Makeunion and a viral clip for Glasno merch.
Solution
For the clinic: colour and skin, my own subtitles from a Whisper transcript proofread by hand, graphic inserts in place of the old titles, loudness at −14 LUFS. The “no-camera video” is fully AI-made: a doctor avatar in Veo 3.1 with Russian lip sync and a 3D face insert where the volume shift is a morph between Nano Banana frames. For the brands: punchy editing, titles with accent words and motion captions.
Result
The clinic re-edit took about 50 minutes. The no-camera video needed no shoot and fit within Google Flow’s free daily limit.
