Palazzo trousers for Wildberries

Freelance · product card

Brief
The brand Bless You Today needed two main images without infographics, plus shots for the photo funnel. The condition: fabric, colour and hardware stay exactly as in the source photos. A separate task was a trench coat: recolour it to a swatch and remove an extra seam.
What I did
I looked at competitors first. Almost everyone shoots palazzo trousers on grey with a big caption, so the feed turns into one grey blur. I made both main images full-length and without text: one in a light studio, one on an old-town street. AI generated the scene and the top, and I moved the trousers over from the source photo and matched colour and light by hand. Versions where the AI narrowed the legs went in the bin. The funnel has four shots: volume and fit, details, back and side, three outfits. The coat was recoloured through a mask, so folds and texture stayed where they were.
Outcome
About seven hours in total. The trousers in every frame are the real ones from the source photos.

Reels for brands and experts

Freelance · editing

Brief
Five videos for different clients: an aesthetic medicine clinic, the embroidery brand BVRLY, Makeunion and Glasno merch.
What I did
For the clinic I rebuilt an existing doctor video: fixed the skin tone, toned down a blown-out window, replaced jumpy titles with my own (Whisper transcript, then proofread by hand) and levelled the sound to -14 LUFS. The second clinic video was made without a camera. The doctor avatar was built in Veo 3.1 and speaks Russian in sync, and the 3D insert about how a face ages is a morph between two images. For the brands it's regular fast editing: titles with highlighted words, captions, cuts that follow the speech.
Outcome
The re-edit took about fifty minutes. The no-camera video fit into Google Flow's free daily limit.
